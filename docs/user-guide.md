# BrAInBox – student app user guide

BrAInBox is a study app for G.C.E. A/L Biology students. It finds the topics
you are weak in, explains them, and gives you practice until they are strong.

This guide walks through the app in the order a new student meets it.
Screenshots are marked **[Screenshot: …]**; replace each with a real capture
before you print or share the guide.

## 1. Install and open the app

- BrAInBox runs on **Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer** and needs an internet connection.
- Your school gives you the app as an APK file or a link. Open it on your
  phone and tap **Install**. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps"
  for your browser or file manager (only for this install).
- The first time you open BrAInBox you see a short welcome tour. Swipe
  through it or tap **Skip**.

**[Screenshot: welcome tour, first page]**

## 2. Create an account and sign in

### Sign up

1. Tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password. The password needs **at least 8
   characters with a letter and a number**; the bar under it shows how strong
   it is.
3. Tick **I agree to how my learning data is used** (tap *Privacy policy*
   to read it first).
4. Tap **Create account**. BrAInBox emails you a **6-digit code**. Type it
   in to confirm your email.

You can also tap **Continue with Google** to use your Google account instead.

**[Screenshot: sign up screen]**  **[Screenshot: enter the 6-digit code]**

### Sign in and forgotten passwords

- **Log in** with your email and password, or with Google.
- **Forgot password?** sends a code to your email; enter it, then choose a
  new password.
- The code did not arrive? Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then tap
  **Resend**.

## 3. Set up your profile

After your first sign-in BrAInBox asks a few questions so it can plan for you:

| Step | What you choose |
|---|---|
| Stream and subject | Biological Science · Biology (other subjects show *Coming soon*) |
| Goals | Your A/L exam year, the grade you aim for, how confident you feel |
| Study time | Which days and what time of day you usually study, and for how long |

You can change all of this later in **Settings → Edit profile**.

**[Screenshot: profile setup, goals step]**

## 4. The diagnostic test

Next comes a short **diagnostic**: a quick check across all 10 units, so your
plan starts where you actually are. It is **not graded**.

- Tap **Start diagnostic** and answer each question. Skip a question if you
  have no idea; that is useful information too.
- At the end you see **Your starting point**: your strong and weak units.
- **Skip for now** takes you straight to Home. You can take the diagnostic
  later from Home.

**[Screenshot: Your starting point]**

## 5. Home

Home is your daily starting point.

- **Next best action**: the one thing BrAInBox thinks you should do now,
  for example "Practise Mendelian inheritance".
- **Today's plan**: short tasks built from your weak topics and study time.
- Your **streak** and recent progress.
- Tap your **avatar** to open *My profile*: stats, achievements and your goal.
- The **bell** opens your notifications.

The bottom bar takes you to the six main areas: **Home, Learn, Practice,
Insights, Groups, Settings**.

**[Screenshot: Home]**

## 6. Learn

Learn follows the A/L syllabus: **Unit → Topic**.

1. Pick a unit to see its topics and your mastery for each one.
2. Open a topic. Its tabs give you:
   - **Notes**: full notes for the topic. Tap **Short notes** for a
     one-page summary for revision.
   - **Diagrams**: labelled diagrams. Tap one to open it full screen and
     pinch to zoom. **Generate a new visual** asks the AI to draw one.
   - **Practice**: start a quiz on just this topic.
3. Tap the **bookmark** to save notes to **My library**.

### Your own notes

**My uploaded notes** lets you upload a PDF or a photo of your class notes.
BrAInBox reads them and gives you a summary. Uploads stay private to you.

### My visuals

**My visuals** lets you ask for a diagram about anything, for example
"Krebs cycle". The visuals you make are kept on your phone.

## 7. Practice

The Practice tab shows a quiz in progress, your recent results, and how many
questions you got wrong.

- **Topic quiz**: MCQs from one topic. In **Quiz setup** choose the
  unit, difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard, Mixed), number of questions and
  whether it is **Timed**, then tap **Start quiz**.
- **Focus on my weak areas**: questions from your weakest areas first.
- **Practice my mistakes**: the questions you answered wrongly.
- **Resume quiz** continues a quiz where you stopped.

After each answer you see whether it was right, **why**, and, when you chose a
wrong option, the **misconception** behind it. At the end you get your score
and what to do next.

## 8. Exams and written answers

### Past papers and model papers

- **Past papers**: real A/L papers to do as a timed exam. Questions that
  no longer match the current syllabus are marked *Outdated syllabus*; you can
  hide them.
- **Generate a model paper**: the AI builds a new A/L-style paper for the
  units you choose.
- In **exam mode** a timer runs. **Leave** asks before it ends the exam. At
  the end you see your marks per question.

### Structured and essay questions

Under **Structured and essay practice** you answer written questions:

- **Type your answer**, or
- **Photograph my handwritten answer**: photograph each page flat, in good
  light. BrAInBox shows you the text it read from your photos. Fix any words
  it misread, then confirm to get your marks.

The feedback shows the marking points you covered, the ones you missed, and an
estimated mark.

## 9. Insights

Insights shows how you are doing:

- **Overview**: your overall mastery and how many topics are *Mastered*,
  *Strong*, *Improving* or *Weak*. Each status has its own icon, word and
  colour.
- **Mastery map**: a heatmap of every topic. Tap a topic for details.
- **Topic detail**: your results over time and the concepts to work on.
- **Mistake journal**: every question you got wrong, with the answer you
  picked and the likely misconception. Tap **Mark fixed** once you understand
  it, or **Practice my mistakes** to try them again.
- **Next steps / Study plan**: recommendations for what to study, and a
  weekly plan.

## 10. Groups (community)

Groups are study groups where students help each other.

- Everyone is in **General**. There is one group per unit; groups for your
  weak units are listed first.
- Tap **Join** or **Leave** on a group.
- Open a group to read posts. Tap **New post** to write one (join the
  group first); tap a post to read and add replies.
- Use the **More (⋮)** menu on a post to **report** it (spam, rude, wrong
  information…). Admins review every report.
- By default you appear as "A/L student". Turn on **Settings → Show my name in
  community** to show your name.

Be kind and stay on topic. Posts that break the rules are removed.

## 12. Privacy and your data

**Settings → Data & privacy** shows what BrAInBox keeps and why:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Profile (name, email, phone) | For your account only |
| Study time and activity | To build your weekly plan |
| Answers and results | To find gaps and recommend next steps |
| Uploads and handwriting | Processed to summarise, then stored privately |

Choices you can turn on or off:

- **Personalised recommendations**
- **Use my answers to improve AI feedback** (anonymised)
- **Share anonymised data for education research** (with approved Sri Lankan
  universities)

Your rights:

- **Download my data**: a JSON file with everything BrAInBox keeps about you.
- **Delete my account**: removes all your data. Type **DELETE** to confirm.
  This cannot be undone.

## 13. About AI content

Some notes, diagrams, questions and feedback are made by AI. They always carry
an **AI** badge. AI content is checked automatically and by teachers, but it can
still contain mistakes: if something looks wrong, check your textbook and tell
your teacher or use **Help & feedback**.

## 14. Problems and answers

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "You're offline" | Check Wi-Fi or mobile data. The app retries when you are back online. |
| The 6-digit code didn't arrive | Check spam, wait a minute, tap **Resend**. |
| You can't sign in any more and your password is right | Your account may have been suspended by an admin. Contact your school. |
| AI generation takes long | Notes, papers and visuals can take up to a minute. You can leave the screen; it keeps working. |
| The first screen after a quiet period is slow | The server wakes up after being idle; it takes a few seconds once. |
| Something else | **Settings → Help & feedback**; include the app version from the bottom of Settings. |
